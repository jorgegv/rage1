## THINGS TO INVESTIGATE (future, beyond today)

- **Free the JSP BAT area on CPC.** JSP's BAT (Background Attribute Table) at
  0x5800 (~2 KB up to FTT@0x6000) is meaningless on the CPC: CPC graphics have no
  per-cell attributes (colour is per-pixel in the bitmap), unlike the ZX. So the
  BAT can be dropped from the CPC JSP build and the 0x5800-0x5FFF region reclaimed
  for program/data use — extra page-B budget that directly eases the DATA-in-page-B
  pressure. Investigate compiling BAT out of the CPC path and shifting/reclaiming
  the JSP block.
- (Resolved/moot) the old "6 KB DSBUF cap" trap: the reorder enlarges DSBUF to
  10 KB, so the cap is gone (a build-time overflow guard for DATA+BSS+stack vs
  DSBUF base is still worth adding).
