## Task 6: datagen full modularization

Stage 2 of the datagen refactor (decision A, Task 5). Split the monolith into
`RAGE::Datagen::*` modules, one at a time.

- Split the monolith (`tools/datagen.pl`, ~5148 lines) into `RAGE::Datagen::*`
  modules: the 1229-line `read_input_data` parser, the ~1500 lines of
  `generate_*` emitters, flow-rules, datasets/codesets, tracker.
- **Prerequisite:** introduce a **context object** to replace the ~30 file-scoped
  globals (e.g. `@h_game_data_lines` — 87 refs; the `@all_*` model arrays + their
  `*_name_to_index` hashes; the emit accumulators). Without it, extracted modules
  just reach back into `main::` and nothing is actually decoupled.
- **Method:** one module at a time, **each extraction gated on `make
  all-test-builds` + `tests/00regression/` green and ZX byte-identical**. NOT a
  big-bang rewrite (avoid large untested modifications).
- Modules under `lib/RAGE/` (RAGE1 convention), per Task 5 decision #1.
- Work in the "datagen_full_modularization" branch.
- Use ultrathink.
